言字 (Yana) 0.4.0：右 ⌘ 成了听写键，新的引导，免费试用，安装包小了很多。

## 新功能

- **右 ⌘ 听写。** 轻点右 ⌘ 开始，再点一下结束；按住说话，松开就完成；连点两下免手持。右 ⌘ 当修饰键用（⌘C、⌘ 点链接）时什么都不会发生。F5 留给系统听写，想继续用 F5 可以在「设置 › 快捷键」里打开。右 ⌘ 已经用来切输入法的，可以把它关掉、改用 F5。0.4.0 之前就装好的保持原来的 F5。
- **新的引导。** 五步，一边走一边演示：言字图标的笔画跟着你的声音动，练习时会显示你说的和插入的有什么不同。语音模型在后台下载，不再单独占一步。
- **免费试用（美国和加拿大）。** 新装的 Mac 不填 Key 也能润色，大约 300 次。润色时文字经言字的服务器转给 OpenAI，不会保存。用完后填上自己的 OpenAI Key 就行。
- **匿名使用统计。** 每天一次：听写次数、字数和免费试用花费，带一个随机编号，从不包含听写内容。可以在「设置 › 历史与隐私」里关掉。

## 改进

- 安装包从约 560 MB 降到约 62 MB，装好后的 App 从 1.2 GB 降到约 310 MB。
- 打开 DMG 就能看到怎么安装：把言字拖到 Applications（应用程序）文件夹。
- 界面里不再出现模型名。
- 用自己的 Key 时，余额用完、Key 无效都会直接说清楚，并给出去充值或修改 Key 的按钮。
- 语音模型下载失败可以在菜单里重试，磁盘空间不够会提前说。
- 没开润色或润色失败时，中文原文也会用全角标点。

## 修复

- 关麦克风时 CoreAudio 卡住，整个 App 冻住、快捷键失灵。现在不会再冻住；如果麦克风真的卡死，会提示「重新打开」。
- 开会或上课时（Zoom、微信占着声音输出），开始听写会把暂停的音乐放出来。现在只有一个通话 App 在出声时不会再按播放/暂停键；关掉「录音时静音其他声音」后完全不碰音乐。
- 系统没法调音量的音箱，每次听写都会在日志里报错；现在直接跳过，设置里会说明原因。

## 安装

下载 `Yana-0.4.0.dmg`，打开后把言字拖进 Applications（应用程序）。已经用 Apple 公证。

需要 Apple 芯片的 Mac（M1 或更新），macOS 13.5 或更高。

**English**

言字 (Yana) 0.4.0: right ⌘ is the dictation key, a new guide, a free trial, and a much smaller download.

**New**

- **Dictate with right ⌘.** Tap to start, tap again to finish; hold to talk and let go to finish; double-tap for hands-free. Used as a modifier (⌘C, ⌘-click) it does nothing. F5 is left to macOS dictation; turn it back on in Settings › Shortcuts. If right ⌘ already switches your input source, turn it off there and use F5. Installs from before 0.4.0 keep F5.
- **A new first-run guide**: five steps that show rather than tell. The 言 icon's strokes move with your voice, and the practice step shows what you said against what went in. The speech model downloads in the background instead of being a step.
- **A free trial (US and Canada)**: about 300 refinements on a new Mac with no key. Your text passes through our server to OpenAI and is not stored. Afterwards, add your own OpenAI key.
- **Anonymous usage stats**, once a day: dictation and character counts and free-trial spend, with a random ID, never what you dictated. Turn them off in Settings › History & Privacy.

**Improved**

- The download is about 62 MB (was about 560 MB), and the installed app about 310 MB (was 1.2 GB).
- The disk image shows how to install: drag Yana onto Applications.
- No model names in the interface.
- With your own key, running out of credit or a rejected key is said plainly, with a button to fix it.
- A failed speech-model download can be retried from the menu; too little disk space is caught first.
- Raw Chinese transcripts get full-width punctuation.

**Fixed**

- The app could freeze, shortcut and all, when CoreAudio hung stopping the microphone. It no longer freezes, and a microphone that is truly stuck offers a relaunch.
- In a Zoom or WeChat call, starting a dictation could start paused music. With only a call holding output, play/pause is no longer pressed; with "mute other sound while recording" off, music is never touched.
- A speaker with no software volume logged an error on every dictation; it is now skipped, and Settings explains why.

Drag Yana into Applications. Needs a Mac with Apple silicon on macOS 13.5 or later.
